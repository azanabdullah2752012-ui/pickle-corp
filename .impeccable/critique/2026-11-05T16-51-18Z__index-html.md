#### Overall Impression
A visually captivating, high-craft studio experience with remarkable tactile charisma. The biggest opportunity is eliminating micro-interaction friction (fixing the broken trade proposal prefill, keyboard inoperability, spacebar scroll trap) and reducing extraneous decorative shelf clutter so the actual shipped software and 3D card shine.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Broken "Propose Trade" Quick-Fill Flow**
  - **What**: Clicking "Propose trade" on favor pills fails to prefill the message form because `script.js` targets `#contact-message` while `index.html` defines `#contact-favor` and `#contact-idea`.
  - **Why it matters**: Breaks the primary conversion funnel of the site.
  - **Fix**: Update `script.js` to populate `#contact-favor` and `#contact-idea` and shift focus.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] Keyboard Inoperability on Custom Interactive Controls**
  - **What**: Interactive elements with `role="button"` (`#hero-chai-mug`, `.vignette-prop`) lack `keydown` handlers for Enter and Spacebar.
  - **Why it matters**: Violates WCAG 2.1 SC 2.1.1; keyboard-only users cannot activate controls.
  - **Fix**: Add Enter/Space key listeners or convert to semantic `<button type="button">`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

- **[P1] Footer Dead Link to Non-Existent `#services`**
  - **What**: `<a href="#services">What We Do</a>` points to a non-existent anchor.
  - **Why it matters**: Degrades trust and signals broken QA.
  - **Fix**: Point anchor to `#philosophy` or `#work`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Global Spacebar Hijacking by 3D Card**
  - **What**: Window keydown intercepts `Space` to flip the 3D card whenever it is in the viewport, blocking natural page scrolling.
  - **Why it matters**: Traps keyboard users and breaks expected browser navigation.
  - **Fix**: Restrict Spacebar flip strictly to when the card container has active `:focus-visible`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Form Placeholder & Plaque Contrast Deficits**
  - **What**: `span.plaque-sub` (3.9:1) and `::placeholder` (4.0:1) fall just below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 requirement.
  - **Why it matters**: Low readability in bright environments.
  - **Fix**: Darken plaque subtext to `#2e1a04` (>6:1) and placeholder text to `#5f574d` (>5:1).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P3] Trailing Line Straggler & Repetitive Shelves**
  - **What**: Line orphan in project card description ("encryption."); 6 identical wooden shelf vignettes add visual fatigue.
  - **Why it matters**: Elevated cognitive noise floor.
  - **Fix**: Add `text-wrap: pretty;` and distill intermediate shelves so Hero Desk and 3D Card dominate.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`
