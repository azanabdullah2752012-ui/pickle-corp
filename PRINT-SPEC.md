### 1. General Job Overview

- **Client / Studio**: Pickle Corp™ (Azan & Thanvi • Marketing: Viren)
- **Product**: 2-Sided Premium Business Card / Promissory Barter Marker
- **Dimensions (Finished Trim Size)**: `3.5 in × 2.0 in` (`88.9 mm × 50.8 mm`) — Standard US / International Business Card
- **Full Bleed Size**: `3.75 in × 2.25 in` (`95.25 mm × 57.15 mm`) — Includes `0.125 in` (`3.175 mm`) bleed margin on all 4 edges
- **Safe Zone**: `3.25 in × 1.75 in` (`82.55 mm × 44.45 mm`) — Keep all crucial text and logos inside this boundary
- **Color Format**: CMYK (Process) or Pantone Spot Match
- **Resolution**: 300+ DPI (Vector preferred for text and line art)
- **Orientation**: Horizontal / Landscape
- **Sides**: 2-Sided (4/4 Full Color Front and Back)
- **Recommended Quantity**: 100 / 250 / 500 cards

---

### 2. Paper Stock & Finishing Requirements

- **Paper Stock**: 
  - **Option A (Recommended - Premium Editorial)**: 350gsm to 450gsm Heavy Cotton Uncoated Matte Cardstock (Warm Natural Off-White / Bone, soft tactile paper tooth matching editorial letterpress).
  - **Option B (Modern Silk)**: 400gsm Heavy Silk / Velvet Matte Laminate with Anti-Scuff protection.
- **Finishes & Embellishments**:
  - **Front Face**: Blind Deboss (deep tactile indentation) on the Pickle Corp Logotype and Torch Mark.
  - **Optional Luxury Accent**: Subtle Metallic Warm Gold / Bronze Foil stamping on the Torch flame.
  - **Back Face**: Crisp black/deep espresso offset printing with high-contrast scannable QR code.
- **Corner Cutting**: Standard 90° Clean Square Trim (or optional subtle 3.5mm rounded radius).
