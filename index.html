Our Approach

How we actually work.

"No scrums. No decks. Just three people and a clear idea of what matters."

01
Understand

Your idea, clearly.

We start by understanding what you're actually trying to do — not just what you're asking for. What's the goal? Who's the audience? What does success look like for this specific project?

02
Agree

On scope and the favour.

We define exactly what we're building — and we agree on a fair favour in return. No ambiguity. No surprises. If the scope changes, we talk about it.

03
Build & ship

Something that works.

We build it properly — accessible, responsive, performant. We test it. We hand it over with documentation so you actually understand what you've got.

The Favour System

No invoices.
No retainers.
No nonsense.

Pickle Corp operates on a favour-based model. Instead of a price list, we agree on a mutual contribution — something that feels fair to both sides.

"The best projects don't start with a quote. They start with a conversation."
01

You explain your idea

Tell us what you're building, who it's for, and what you need. The more context, the better the conversation.

02

We discuss it together

We ask questions. We define what's realistic. We figure out what a good outcome looks like — before anyone agrees to anything.

03

We agree on a favour

Not money. Something genuinely useful — a contribution, a collaboration, spreading the word, or another mutually agreed exchange.

04

We build and hand over

We work on the project according to the agreed scope and hand it over with the context you need to actually use it.
